# Delete your SpeakPro account

**App:** SpeakPro (`com.valuepattern.speakpro`)  
**Publisher:** Value Pattern Labs

You can delete your SpeakPro account, and everything Value Pattern Labs keeps for it, at any time.

**If you subscribe to Pro, cancel the subscription first.** Deleting your account doesn't cancel it. Cancel it in
Google Play under **Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions**, or with **Manage subscription** in SpeakPro's Settings.

## In the app

1. Open SpeakPro and tap the profile icon at the top right of **Practice** to open **Profile and settings**.
2. Scroll to **Your data** and tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm. The account is deleted right away and you are signed out on every device.

To keep your account but remove what our server holds, tap **Delete my data on the server** instead.

## Without the app

Email [support@valuepattern.com](mailto:support@valuepattern.com?subject=Delete%20my%20SpeakPro%20account) from the address you signed in with, or include your SpeakPro username. We confirm the request and delete the account within 7 days.

## What is deleted

- Your account: your username and password hash, or your Google sign-in (email address and name).
- The transcripts of your speeches and negotiations, their word timings, and the AI feedback on them.
- The record of AI requests made for your account.
- Your sign-ins on every device.
- The record of your Pro subscription (its plan, status and dates) and any Pro given to your account by our staff.

Recordings are never stored on our server, so there are none to delete. Practice sessions and recordings kept on your phone are not touched; remove them in the app with **Delete everything on this phone**, or by uninstalling SpeakPro.

## What is kept

Nothing that identifies you:

- A code computed from your Google account ID or username with a secret key, which can't be turned back into either, with how many free seconds of recording you had used. It is kept for one year, so deleting an account and signing up again doesn't reset the free minutes.
- Daily usage totals (how many AI requests were made and what they cost us), no longer linked to you.
- Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases under Google's policies.

Transcripts and feedback are deleted automatically 90 days after a speech even if you keep your account.
