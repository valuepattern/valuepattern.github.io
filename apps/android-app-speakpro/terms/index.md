## What Stage Sensei does

Stage Sensei records your speeches and negotiation practice and gives you feedback. Timing and some measurements are done
on your phone. Transcripts, scores, critiques, suggested rewrites and the other side in a negotiation come from AI.

AI feedback can be wrong, incomplete or a poor fit for your situation. It is practice feedback, not professional,
legal, medical, career or financial advice. Use your own judgment before relying on it.

## Free plan and Pro

- **Free:** practice and on-phone measurements without limit, and AI review of 5 minutes of recordings in total,
  negotiation practice included. Reviews on the free plan show the most important critiques; the rest are counted
  but locked.
- **Pro:** AI review, negotiation practice included, of up to 30 minutes of recordings a day, with every critique
  and rewrite.

Daily limits reset at midnight UTC. We may limit or pause AI review to stop abuse or keep the service running, for
example when an account makes unusual numbers of requests.
